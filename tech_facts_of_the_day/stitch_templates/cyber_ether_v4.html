Microservices Architecture

An architectural style that structures an application as a collection of services that are highly maintainable, loosely coupled, and independently deployable.

add_circle Benefits

Scalability, agility, and easier tech stack diversity across teams.

warning Challenges

Operational complexity, distributed logging, and network latency.

rule Isolation

Each service owns its own database for true decoupling.

trending_up Impact

Reduces average deployment time by up to 60% in large teams.
