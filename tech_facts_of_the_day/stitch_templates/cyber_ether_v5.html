Horizontal vs. Vertical Scaling

SQL databases are typically vertically scalable, meaning you can increase the load on a single server by increasing components like RAM or CPU.

NoSQL databases are horizontally scalable. They handle more traffic by sharding or adding more servers in your database cluster.

NoSQL is often preferred for large or ever-evolving data sets.
